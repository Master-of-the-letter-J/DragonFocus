Summary
Everyday, you have 2 surveys: one to fill in goals to accomplish, and another in the evening of completing them. You can also check them throughout the day. By completing goals, you change the status of a ‘dragon’ — it’s happiness , health, and age.


* Lore: You’re Chosen By a Secret Government to Raise an Ancient Cosmic Dragon Producing Energy for the will of your country, at the expense of being in a cult to be well-fed and happy. Your goal is to raise it to the highest age possible (1 age = 1 day). This ancient dragon has a rage bar, which if it fills up, it starts attacking Civilization, having unprecedented costs to your energy production. Your country must perform a balancing act, to contain this dragon secretly, while producing as much energy to become an ultimate superpower. Your goal is to maintain balance, raise your dragon, and unlock deep secrets, all through deep focus.
   * Early Into App: Unlock Milestones to Unlock New Features and Reveal Secret Government Logs
   * Mid Into App: Continuously Transcend, Complete Goals, Gain Exponential Energy, and Potential Missions to Keep Your Dragon Alive.
   * Late Into App: Keep the Dragon Alive as Long as Possible with Harder and Harder Missions
* Function: To give motivation for achieving goals.
   * Surveys: There’s a “Morning Briefing”, and “Debrief”, which are both editable. Fill these in to review goals at start and end of the day.
   * Goals: Each goal has a title, and can have a description. They Can Have Sub-Goals. You can choose a Goal to Repeat (A “Habitual Goal”), and choose the days of week it repeats (custom, weekends, or weekdays option). Or you can have it to not repeat (A “To-Do Goal”), Setting the Date to be Completed (default Today). Lastly, You Can set a Start Time, End Time, and The Duration it Takes for both goals, or due ‘end of the day’ (each updating in a clean ui), or have a check for no due date. You Can Add a Category Flair, and an Importance Flair to each goal.
   * Types of Goals (Top to Bottom): Late To-Do Goals (ONLY if not completed by end of day), Habit Goals Due Today, To-Do Goals Due Today, Future Habit Goals, To-Do Goals Due 1-7 Days, To-Do Goals Due >7 Days.
   * Non-Premium Goals: Up to 10 Goals, Up to 5 Repeating Goals, 5 Sub-Goals/Goal.
   * Premium Goals: Unlimited Goals, Unlimited Repeating Goals, Unlimited Sub-Goals
   * Settings: You can change settings to make the app less gamified.
   * Pomodoro Timer: The Primary Way to Focus on Goals, Gives a Massive Necessary Boost.
* The Motivation: Unlocking All-Round Secrets. (Stats)
   * Energy: The Primary Currency. You Gain Cosmic Energy Through Generators that harvest the dragon, Which are Boosted Through Pomodoro Timers. Which are Bought Through More Cosmic Energy.
   * Dark Energy: The Secondary Currency. Rarer, Used to Buy Advanced Armies and For Boosts.
   * Crimson Shards: The In-App Purchase Currency. Used to Unlock New Features. You Gain By Milestones, Focus Sessions, and Surveys.
   * Plasma: Prestige Currency With Ascension of Energy. Used to Buy Massive Powers and Boosts Upgrades 
   * Anomalies: Prestige Currency With Transcendence of Plasma. Resets Generators. Used to Buy Ultimate Deities
   * World Population: Max Energy = Current World Population. World Population has a Cap, Which Can Only Be Increased During Transcension.
   * Dragon’s Fury: When Dragon Gets Angry (Percentage Bar) >100, It Unleashes It on The Population. The Dragon Will Succumb To Death if The Population Reaches 0, Since it Gathers its Energy From Population.
* Completion: The Milestones For Completions
   * Ascension (or Armaggedon): After Reaching An Energy Threshold, You Unlock Ascension. This Allows you To Harvest Plasma From Energy Resetting the World’s Energy in An Apocalypse. Plasma Gives Powerful Boosts and Powers That Accelerate Dark Energy Earned, And Therefore Energy Itself.
   * Transcension (or Ascension): Unlocked After Reaching Max Energy and Population Cap, You Unlock Transcension. These Reset Your World and Give You Anomalies Depending on the Total Plasma Gained, Which Are Used to Fund Dragon Deities - The Final Ultimate Feature Allowing for More Control Over the App and Features and Boosts. The Dragon Gives You Secrets on the Origin of the Universe.
* Account, Logs, and Monetization: Details for Account Page
   * The Dragon Pact: A Subscription for More tools to make Scheduling, to Make Progression Faster, and to Buy In-Game Currency (Crimson Shards) For a Discount
   * Logs: Can be Viewed in a Table or List Format, Displaying Results of Surveys For Each Day
   * Achievements and Statstics: Shows Achievements and Statstics
   * Graveyard Page: Shows all Deceased Dragons From…Unfortunate Mishaps.
   * Black Market: Use Money to Buy Crimson Shards. Use Crimson Shards to Unlock Features, or Buy Additional Energy, Orbs, or Boosts.
* General Layout: 4 Tabs. All Pages Have a Top Panel.
   * A Top Panel: For All Pages
   * The Moon Lair: Displays the Dragon, Goals, and the Pomodoro Timer. Everything Focus-Related
   * The World: With the “Game” Aspects (Dragon Guards vs Obsidian League, Ascension, Transcension), and the Market and Black Market.
   * The Dragon Pact: Shows Info to Buy Premium (the Dragon Pact), and The Profile Page (The Dragon Pact) / The Archives in table of list format, Achievements, Statistics A Graveyard)
   * Settings/Options Page: Shows settings for the survey and general settings

The Nexus & Dragon
All of these are adjustable and can be toggled on / off based on the user’s desire!


The Nexus:
* At the very start of your journey, messages will pop-up to spawn the dragon, and give a little background info (keep the user guessing)
* Displays Your Dragon at Center, it’s Name, Fury Bar, and Age (It Dies When World Pop ≤ 0)
* Shows the Bottom Buffer In Bottom of Screen, Scroll to See Previews of:
   1. The Check-In and Check-Out Survey (First, Always!)
   2. Preview Incomplete Goals, Click for Full Page
   3. Preview of Completed Goals, Click for Full Page
   4. Preview of Pomodoro Timer and 4 Bonus Rewards, Click for Full Page
* Click Dragon to Show Quote Texts Each Click!


The Dragon Age & Stages
* Design: A Mysterious (Default Red) Dragon You Must Keep In Touch. As it Ages, its Sprite Changes, and The Math for Population Destroyed Changes…
* Age 0-10: Egg ~ The Dragon Starts off as An Egg, Which Isn’t Yet Hatched
* Age 10-20: Hatchling ~ An Egg With a Baby Dragonet’s Head
* Age 20-30: Dragonet ~ A Full Baby Dragon-Net
* Age 30-90: Juvinelle  ~ A More Grown Dragon Teenager. If It’s a Juvinelle…It can fly
* Age 90-180: Young Adult ~ A Small Dragon That Can Fly. And Breath Fire.
* Age 180-270: Adult ~ A Dragon, Flying, and Breathing Tons of Fire. Fire has Tremors
* Age 270-365: Elder Dragon ~ a Dragon, Flying, and Breaths Blue Fire. Scales are Large, and Tremors +
* Age 365+: Great Wyrm (You Can Change the Name of the Dragon) (Max HP: 500) ~ a Massive Dragon, Small Wings, Breathing Red Fire With  Tremors++


Dragon’s Fury / Anger Level (Milestone 1)
* The Dragon gets furious when the user misses goals or neglects the app, as shown by a Fury Bar Displayed Below It (Goes up to Threshold)
* Threshold: The Default Threshold is (100 + Dragon’s Age). When Anger Exceeds This Amount, the Population Starts to Decrease…
* Min/Max: The Minimum Anger is 0, and the Default Maximum is 10 * Threshold (Increases in Hard and Hard+ Modes)
* Shields: Buy Anger Shields In the Black Market. These shields from anger increase temporarily. 
* When Dragon Gets Angry It Unleashes It on The Population. World Deaths/Day Are Below:
   * +(Dragon’s Total Fury) * (Dragon’s Age)^2 (Max. 1 Billion / Day)
   * +(Dragon’s Total Fury  * 0.002%) * (Current Population).
* Hard Mode: In the options, Hard and Hard+ Modes Increases the Anger Gotten AND Max Anger Significantly, With Trade-Off of More Energy
* Invincible and Lock-In Modes: Anger is Paused in These Modes, With Debuffs to Energy or a Change of Goals.
* (Except for Hard and Hard+ Modes) Click Fury Bar to Decrease It (Price: 1 Fury = 1 Shard)


The Dragon Death
* The Dragon Will Succumbs to death when the Population Reaches 0. A series of messages pops up, revealing how the dragon gets energy from the population.
* Containment Breach: Energy Cannot be Generated, Buffs are Disabled, and You Cannot Access World Tab When Dragon Is Dead
* Mass Destruction: A THIRD of all Base Energy Production is Destroyed (Less is Destroyed with Dark Energy Upgrades and Hepheastus Levels)
* Revive: Revive the Dragon By By Clicking The ‘Revive’ Button, Reviving Instantly
* Revive Note: Population Increases By +1/Dark Energy, Alongside the Bonus Growth % Rate.
* Revive Note: Your Dead Dragon Will Show Up on a Grave in The Dragon Graveyard
* Note: (Regardless of If Dragon is Dead) You Get +3, +2, And Then +1 Random Single-Hour Snack For Every 7 Days Your Are Offline (See Black Market Page For Info)

The Surveys
There are two optional surveys: check-in (briefing) and check-out (debriefing) surveys, to guide your with getting rewards through goals


The Survey Process:
* First, Fill in Check-In Survey (Can be Required or Not, Take Again to Change Answers)
* After, Check-Out Survey Will Be Available Until End of Day. (Also Can be Required or Not)
* Once Check-Out is Filled, Check-Out is Disabled Until Another Check-In is Filled in Again


Overview of Survey (Summary): 
1. Survey Advice: Gives Advice About Survey or a Quote (Optional)
2. Mood Question: How do you feel? (Optional, Recommended)
3. Edit: Check In: Edit Your Goals OR Check-Out: Check Off Your Goals and Harvest Rewards.
4. Results: Show the Results, and Rewards


Overview of Survey (Detailed):
1. Survey Advice: Gives Advice About Survey or Quotes
   1. Gives Encouragement To Fill the Survey!
2. Question: How Do You Feel? (Mood Question)
   1. Survey: Optional Morning and Night
   2. Mood: A Question about your mood, affecting the entity’s current character.
      1. Description: Multiple Choice Question About Your Current Mood. Gives -5, -3, 0, +3, and +5 Fury depending on the answer.
         1. High Energy: Excited, Surprised, Shocked, Enraged / Disgusted 
         2. Medium Energy: Fulfilled, Calm / Content, Uneasy / Confused, Anxious
         3. Low Energy: Serene, Sleepy, Bored / Drained, Despair
         4. Basic: Great, Good, Okay, Meh, Down, Sad, Angry
3. Question: Edit Goals / Check off Goals (Check in or Check Out Survey)
   1. Show the Goal Editor in Its Entirety in Check In Survey
   2. Shows the Complete Goals Entirely in Check Out Survey.
4. Question: Harvest Goals! (Check Out Survey Only)
   1. Shows All Completed Goals to Confirm A Harvest.
5. Results: Show the Results
   1. All Results will Show Up in a Table in the “Archives” Page (In a New Format!)

Goals: Information


Goals Layout (Incomplete Goals Page)
* Layout: Habit Goal Title On Top. Then, Habit Advice as Subheader. Top Right Has the Add Goals Button. Below, Will Be a List of Goals.
* Goal Limit: 6 Habit Goals, 12 Tasks, No Crimson Challenge Without Premium, Unlimited of all With Premium.
* Two Types - Habits and Tasks
* Sorting: Time to Complete


Individual Goals Layout
* Individual Goal (Left to Right): Checkbox, Time Goal, Title (Subtitle Below), Category, Importance, Crimson Streak / Enable Challenge / Repair Streak, Detail-Edit Goal, Delete Goal, Move Goal.
* Sub-Goals: Listed Below an Individual Goal (Left to Right): Checkbox, Title, (Left), Edit, Delete. 
* Goal Categories: Personal, Mental, Physical, Career, Relationships, Contribution, Financial, Other, Custom
* Goal Importance Levels: Important+, Important, Not Important (Default)
* Goal Time Limits: (Habit) Days of Week Enabled (Default all Selected), Repeat Every _ Weeks (Default 1), (All) Add Sub-Goals, Title, Default Time Taken, and Time Goal is Due. 


Crimson Streaks and Challenge
* Crimson Streak: Repeating Habit Goals Get +1 Streak When Completed Within 24 Hours of Due Time. Between 24-48 Hours, It’s Dormant (Streak Stays Same When Earned). Between 48-72 Hours (Or Number Increased By a Deity) The Streak Cracks, and You Can Pay 5 Shards to Recover the Cracked Streak. After This, It Becomes a Broken Streak, Contributing Cumulative +(Days Broken) Dragon Anger Daily.
   * Total Crimson Streak = Sum of All of The Non-Dormant Streaks. When 0, Dragon Gets Angry…
* Crimson Challenge (Premium Only): Enabling Challenge Mode Give a Cumulative (Doubled) Dark Energy Reward When Accomplishing 3, 7, 14, 30, 90 Days of Streak, But if you Fail (Broken), You Get No Dark Energy and Cumulative (Doubled) Amounts of Dragon Anger!


Check-In/Out Rewards, Harvesting Goals Rewards 
Note: Premium Quadruples The Maximum Shards
Note: Plasma Boosts Increase the Dark Energy Amounts Further!
Note: Check-In and Check-Out Surveys are Optional, Except for Hard+ Mode!


Goals Layout (Completed Goals Page)
* Completion: Goals are added to the completed goals page, have their streaks added, and added to the archives!
* Un-Complete: For goals completed within 24 hours, they can be re-added back to incomplete goals page, (removes added streak, potentially into late goals)
* Finality: When harvested, rewards are calculated based on the day they were checked off, and your current upgrade multipliers. The goal is permanently archived.
* Auto-Harvest: You can enable Auto-Harvest, which harvests goals every 24 hours at the end of the day!
* Lock-In Exception: Goals cannot be harvested or auto-harvested during the lock-in modes.


Anti-Cheat
* Anti-Cheat: All incomplete goals count to the goal limit. All completed goals (harvested and un-harvested) count to the goal limit if it was created on the same day. Deleting a goal removes it from this limit. A goal not created on the same day and harvested does not count towards the limit.
* Anti-Cheat: Goals Completed Within 15 minutes is an “Emergency Directive”. After 3 of These are Done In a Day, No Rewards are Collected.
* Anti-Cheat: Due Date of Late Goals Cannot be Changed!
* Anti-Cheat: Auto-Harvest Goals At the End of the Day, Showing the Rewards When User Comes Back


Rewards
* Check In Reward: +(20 + 10 * Streak) Dark Energy, +(1 + Streak, Up to 5) Shards (Max. 5 Shards/Day)
* Check Out Reward: +(20 + 10 * Streak) Dark Energy, +(1 + Streak, Up to 5) Shards (Max. 5 Shards/Day)
* Bonus Pomodoro Reward (Doing 15, 30, 45, 60 min / Day): +(15 Dark Energy + 7.5 * Streak) Dark Energy, +(Streak, Up to 4) Shards (Max. 16 Shards/Day)
* Habit Reward: +(10 Dark Energy + 5 * Streak) Dark Energy, +(Streak, Up to 3) Shards (Max. 30 Shards/Day)
* To-Do Reward: +(10 + # Days To Complete, up to 90), +1 Shards (Max. 10 Shards/Day)
* Sub-Goal Reward: +2 Dark Energy


* Late Reward (Both): +5 Dark Energy, (Repairs Streak automatically if <48 Hours, But No Reward)
* During Challenge: No Dark Energy Reward (Doubled Cumulative When Completed Challenge), Keep Shard Reward
* Repair Streak 48-72 +(Hestia Level * 24) Hours: 5 Shard Cost.


* Fury Increase: Every Day Broken Streak = +(# Days Broken) Anger. Every To-Do Missed = +1 Anger. Miss Survey (Hard modes only)  = +2 Anger.
* Fury Decrease: Every Shard Earned From Tasks and Surveys = -2 Anger/Shard.
* Fury Decrease: Every To-Do or Habit Completed Also Contributes to -2 Anger.
* Fury Decrease: Every Sub-Goal Completed Also Contributes to -1 Anger.

The Pomodoro Cave / Timer / Other Rewards
The Pomodoro Timer Enters “Containment” For The Dragon, Giving Rapid Increases in Rewards. This is the best way to get pure energy.
All Other Rewards are Counted as Fractions of The Pomodoro Rewards, So They Also Get Buffed By The Plasma Upgrades!


The Pomodoro Timer: Settings
* Timer Type: Active, Short Break (5 min), Long Break (15+ min) (On Top, Timer Can be Paused or Continued)
* Timer: Shows the Main Timer in a Circle. Also Shows the Energy Reward.
* Intervals: 10 Min, 1 Min, 10 Seconds (+/-). In a Row Below the Timer.
* Boosts: On Left Button, Choose a Boost to Activate (Cannot be Changed During Session)
* Pause: On Center Button, Pause the Timer for Certain Time, Activating a Short or Long Break.
* Break Containment (End): On Smaller Right Button, End the Timer, Stop Session, Show Rewards.
* Goals: Choose Goal(s) to Be Listed on the Timer, Scrollable Below the 3 Buttons.
   * When All Checked off, Shows the Remaining Goals
* Bonus Rewards: Below Goals, There are 4 Bonus Optional Daily Streak-able Rewards for 15, 30, 45, and 60 minutes on timer.


Pomodoro Rewards (Default):
Cumulated Through Multiple Pomodoro Sessions. 


* Energy: ×100 Energy Production

Purpose
* When Energy Exceeds Population, It Gets Nerfed to a Massive 1% of Production.
* The Max Energy in A Transcension is the Population Cap. When Reached, You Must Transcend.
* When Population Reaches 0, The Dragon Must Be Revived; Energy Prod. Stopped 100% During this period
* Default Energy Multiplier = 1 * log10(World Population)
* Armageddon Increases Max Population and Population Growth!


Growth
* The Population Starts at 8 Billion, and Increases at a Default Rate of 1% / 100,000 Seconds. 
* If Population is Below 1 Billion, Rate of Increase Is Increased to 1% / 10,000 Seconds
* If Population is Below 100 Million, Rate of Increase Is Increased to 1% / 1,000 Seconds
* If Population is Below 1 Million, Rate of Increase Is Increased to 1% / 100 Seconds
* If Population is Below 1000, Rate of Increase Is Increased to 1% / 10 Seconds


Max
* Max World Population is 1 Trillion * 1000^(Armageddon Amount) * (Other Factors By Deities)


Deaths
* Deaths are Based on Dragon’s Anger Above Threshold (Default: 10 Times Maximum Anger Capacity)
* World Deaths/Second is:
* +(10 + Dragon’s Fury Above Threshold) * (Dragon’s Age)^2.
* +(0.001% Dragon’s Fury Above Threshold) * (Current Population).
